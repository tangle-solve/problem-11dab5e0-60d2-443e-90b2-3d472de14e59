### CHEM-000428: Solution-phase N₂ capture by a designed silylene–borole frustrated Lewis pair
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Synthesize a predicted silylene–borole pair and demonstrate solution-phase N₂ binding and activation. Establish structural identity, reaction rate, and equilibrium constant.
- **Status:** The 2025 designs predict barriers of 16.9–22.5 kcal/mol and reaction free energies of −32.3 to −36.7 kcal/mol. N₂ cleavage by SiH₂ in a cryogenic matrix was demonstrated in 2024; the designed solution chemistry remains untested.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Tests whether aromaticity-driven cooperativity survives synthesis and solvent effects.
- **Key works:** S. Dong, W. Wang, and J. Zhu (2025), “Design of Borole- and Silylene-Based Frustrated Lewis Pairs for Dinitrogen Activation Promoted by Aromaticity: A Combined DFT and Machine Learning Study,” [DOI: 10.1021/acs.inorgchem.5c02343](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.inorgchem.5c02343); L. Cai et al. (2024), “N2 cleavage by silylene and formation of H2Si(μ-N)2SiH2,” [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-024-48064-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48064-z).
